इस बारे में About Shaurya

Shaurya (शौर्य — valour) is a one-page radio for Indian patriotic music. Press play once and it keeps going: the playlist is shuffled fresh on every visit, quotes from our war heroes rotate on their own, and the background moves through a curated wall of Indian Armed Forces photographs.

It is built as a tribute — to the Army, the Navy and the Air Force, and to everyone who wore the uniform before us.

  • R reshuffle playlist
  • L open playlist
  • S salute

Questions

Where does the music come from?

Every track streams from an embedded YouTube player. Shaurya hosts no audio files and offers no downloads. Video is intentionally hidden — this is a music-only experience.

Can I play my own YouTube playlist?

Yes. Add ?list=YOUR_PLAYLIST_ID to the URL and Shaurya will shuffle that playlist instead of the built-in one. The playlist must be public or unlisted.

Whose photographs are these?

All background photographs are freely licensed images of the Indian Armed Forces from Wikimedia Commons (public domain, Creative Commons, or the Government Open Data Licence – India). The credit for the photo on screen is shown in the bottom corner.

Is there a login, app, or cost?

No. No account, no ads, no paywall — just a page that plays music.
